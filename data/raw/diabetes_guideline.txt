Title: Type 2 Diabetes Management Guideline
Organization: Example Diabetes Association
Publication Year: 2024
Section: 4.2
Document Type: clinical_guideline

Management of type 2 diabetes should include lifestyle modification, glucose monitoring, and individualized treatment planning.

Patients should be encouraged to maintain regular physical activity and follow an appropriate nutrition plan.

Metformin may be considered as an initial pharmacologic therapy for many adults when clinically appropriate.

Treatment decisions should consider kidney function, cardiovascular disease, hypoglycemia risk, and individual patient needs.
