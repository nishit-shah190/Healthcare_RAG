Title: Adult Hypertension Clinical Guideline
Organization: Example Health Institute
Publication Year: 2024
Section: 3.1
Document Type: clinical_guideline

Adults diagnosed with hypertension should receive counseling on lifestyle modification.

Recommended lifestyle interventions include regular physical activity, maintaining a healthy body weight, reducing excessive sodium intake, limiting alcohol consumption, and following a balanced diet rich in fruits and vegetables.

Medication therapy may be considered based on blood pressure severity, cardiovascular risk, age, and coexisting medical conditions.

Patients should have their blood pressure monitored regularly to evaluate treatment effectiveness.
